# Qt licensing

Qt 6 is available under commercial, LGPLv3, and GPLv3 terms, with some modules
available only under GPLv3 in the open-source distribution. The engineering
application must choose and document its license path before shipping binaries.

The planned Widgets/Core/Gui/Concurrent route avoids intentionally selecting a
GPL-only module, but this is not legal advice and does not remove LGPLv3 or
third-party-notice obligations. For proprietary distribution, obtain legal
review or a suitable commercial Qt license. Track the exact Qt version and
ship required notices/SBOM material where applicable.

Authoritative reference: <https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/licensing.html>.
